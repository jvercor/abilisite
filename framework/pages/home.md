# Page Template: Home

## Overview
This document defines the structure, content rules, and SEO requirements for the home page. The home page is the most important page of the website—it must immediately communicate who the client is, what they do, and what the visitor should do next. The agent must follow this structure in order, applying the Design Guidelines, SEO Guidelines, and Content Guidelines throughout.

---

## Page Structure

### 1. Hero Section

The hero is the first thing a visitor sees. It must be visually dominant, immediately clear, and keyword-optimized.

#### H1 — Primary Headline
- **MANDATORY: One H1 per page. This is a strict SEO requirement.**
- The H1 must include the primary keyword of the business
- It should clearly communicate what the company or project does and, where applicable, who it serves or where it operates
- Keep it between 5 and 12 words
- Write for immediate clarity—avoid clever wordplay that sacrifices understanding
- Refer to SEO Guidelines for keyword placement rules

> **Agent instruction**: Use the client's primary keyword and location (if provided) to construct the H1. It must pass the "five second test"—a visitor reading it for five seconds should immediately understand what the business does.

**Examples:**
- "Professional Landscaping Services in Denver, Colorado"
- "Custom Web Design for Small Businesses"
- "Trusted Family Law Attorney in Miami"

#### H2 — Supporting Subtitle
- The H2 supports and expands the H1
- It should function similarly to a meta description—elaborating on the value proposition in one or two sentences
- Include a secondary keyword naturally if possible
- Keep it between 15 and 30 words
- Should speak directly to the target audience and their needs

**Examples:**
- "We help homeowners transform their outdoor spaces into beautiful, low-maintenance gardens they can enjoy year-round."
- "From concept to launch, we build fast, modern websites that help small businesses grow online."

#### CTA Button — Hero
- One primary CTA button must be present in the hero section
- The CTA text must be action-oriented and specific
- The CTA must reflect the client's primary business goal and industry
- Refer to Content Guidelines for CTA copy examples by industry
- The button must link to the contact page or a booking/quote form

---

### 2. About Section

A brief introduction to the company or project that gives the visitor enough context to understand who they are dealing with and builds initial trust.

#### Rules
- This section must not be a full biography—it is a teaser that leads the visitor to the About page
- Length: 2 to 4 sentences maximum
- Must communicate:
  - Who the company is
  - What makes them different or trustworthy
  - A reason to want to learn more
- Must NOT duplicate content from the About page word for word—this is a unique summary
- Tone must match the established brand voice from the Content Guidelines

#### Read More Button
- A button or text link must be present inviting the visitor to the About page
- Suggested copy: "Learn More About Us", "Our Story", "Meet the Team", "About [Company Name]"
- This link must point to the About page

---

### 3. Services Section

A preview of the client's services or products. This section gives visitors an overview of what is offered and directs them to individual service pages for more detail.

#### Section Title
- Use an H2 for the section title
- Suggested titles: "Our Services", "What We Offer", "How We Can Help", or a keyword-optimized variation
- The H2 can include a secondary keyword naturally if appropriate

#### Service Cards
Each service must be presented as a card or block containing:

**Service Title (Clickable Link)**
- The service title must be a clickable link pointing to that service's dedicated page
- The title must follow keyword best practices—it should reflect how customers search for that service
- Example: "Residential Landscaping" not just "Landscaping"
- The title functions as an anchor link and must use descriptive text—never "Click Here"

**Service Description**
- 2 to 3 sentences maximum
- Must be unique content—this description must not appear anywhere else on the website, including the service's own dedicated page
- Should highlight the key benefit or outcome of the service
- Should create enough curiosity to make the visitor want to read more
- Apply the Content Guidelines expansion strategy if the client's description is too short

> **Agent instruction**: Generate a unique short description for each service based on the client's briefing data. Flag to the client that this description is exclusive to the home page and a different expanded version will be used on the service's own page.

#### Number of Services
- Display all services the client has provided
- If the client has more than 6 services, consider grouping related services or discussing with the client whether all should be featured on the home page

---

### 4. Closing CTA Section

A final call to action at the bottom of the home page that captures visitors who have scrolled through the full page but have not yet taken action.

#### Rules
- This section must have an H2 headline that reinforces the value proposition or creates urgency
- It must have a clear CTA button
- The content must feel natural and not repetitive of the hero CTA—different angle, same goal

#### CTA Patterns by Business Goal
The agent must identify the client's primary business goal and select the most appropriate pattern. The selected pattern must be presented to the client for confirmation before finalizing.

| Business Goal | H2 Example | CTA Button Example |
|---|---|---|
| Generate leads | "Ready to Get Started? Let's Talk." | "Request a Free Quote" |
| Book appointments | "Your Next Appointment Is Just a Click Away" | "Book Now" |
| Drive phone calls | "Have Questions? We're Here to Help." | "Call Us Today" |
| Drive store or location visits | "Come See Us In Person" | "Get Directions" |
| Sell products online | "Find Exactly What You're Looking For" | "Shop Now" |
| Build newsletter or community | "Stay in the Loop" | "Subscribe" |

> **Agent instruction**: Based on the client's industry and primary goal collected during briefing, select the most suitable CTA pattern from the table above. Present the choice to the client with a brief explanation of why it was selected, and ask for confirmation or adjustment before generating the final version.

## SEO Checklist for Home Page

Before finalizing the home page, the agent must verify:

- [ ] H1 is present, unique, and includes the primary keyword
- [ ] H2 subtitle supports the H1 and includes a secondary keyword where natural
- [ ] Meta title is between 50 and 60 characters and includes the primary keyword
- [ ] Meta description is between 150 and 160 characters
- [ ] All service titles are descriptive, keyword-aware, and function as internal links to service pages
- [ ] All images have alt text
- [ ] Hero CTA and closing CTA are present and use descriptive, action-oriented text
- [ ] About section links to the About page
- [ ] No content in this page is duplicated from other pages
- [ ] Canonical tag is present
- [ ] Open Graph tags are present

---

## Content Notes

- The home page is the only page where the agent will write brief, unique service descriptions. These descriptions must not be reused on individual service pages.
- The about section on the home page is a teaser only—it must not replace or duplicate the full About page content.
- All content must follow the tone, expansion rules, and writing principles defined in the Content Guidelines.
- All visual structure, spacing, and component rules must follow the Design Guidelines.
